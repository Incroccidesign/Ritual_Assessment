# Ritual — AI Act scope note

Version: 16 September 2026

Ritual currently provides forms, assessment workflows, access management and export/reporting tools. It does **not** use a machine-learning or generative-AI component to infer, generate, rank, recommend or make decisions about people.

Accordingly, the current product is not operated as an AI system within the ordinary scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. This note is not a claim about future versions.

Before introducing any AI feature, including automated summarisation, recommendations, scoring, classification, chatbot assistance or generative content, the operator must:

1. assess whether the feature is an AI system and identify the operator role;
2. assess intended purpose, affected people, data categories, accuracy, human oversight and foreseeable misuse;
3. update the privacy notice, terms, technical documentation and risk controls;
4. determine whether AI Act transparency, risk-management, logging, human-oversight or other obligations apply;
5. obtain specialist legal review before release where the use case could affect people materially.
